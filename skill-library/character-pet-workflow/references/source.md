Source: [“I switched to Claude Code” — The Commons](https://github.com/Collaboration95/common-grounds-makeathon/blob/main/docs/gist-claude-switch.md), Collaboration95/common-grounds-makeathon, retrieved 24 October 2026. Source blob SHA: `387dd0d78a1bf2a4b11ba07011014b4e85e21784`.

The article describes a character pipeline that separates request interpretation, behavior generation, asset generation, and checking. It uses a compact behavior contract, deterministic simulation, a bounded repair attempt, and sprite sanity checks. Its author notes that automated checks did not replace visual review.

This skill adapts those workflow ideas to the user's character-building work, including animated GLB and static DXF delivery. It does not reproduce the article's implementation, remote-server commands, provider configuration, or sample artwork.

The article's performance and cost figures are author-reported results from a particular setup. They are not guarantees or independently verified current pricing. Its screening rules and model choices are project-specific, not general instructions for this skill. Consult current official documentation before any future provider integration, and obtain the user's scope and budget first.
