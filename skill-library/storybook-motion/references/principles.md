# Eight original recipes

| Principle | Recipe | Useful application | Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easing | `ease.soft` | Settle a new chapter gently | No abrupt stop |
| Offset and delay | `stagger` + `reveal` | Introduce related cards in order | All content available without motion |
| Fade | `fade` | Transition scene captions | Old and new text never overlap |
| Transform and morph | `morph`, `drift` | Soft paper silhouette or floating lantern | No layout shift |
| Masking | `mask` | Uncover an illustration | Content remains focusable and understandable |
| Dimension | `dimension.hover` | Small card tilt | Touch and keyboard do not require hover |
| Parallax | `parallax(x,y,depth)` | Move foreground slower than background | Clamp depth and disable for reduced motion |
| Zoom | `zoom` | Slow camera drift over a still illustration | Subject stays within the crop |

The parameters and code are original project resources. Concept reference: https://motion.zajno.com/ (reviewed 24 October 2026). No proprietary demonstration assets are included. A cinematic still-image treatment should be described as scrollytelling rather than as an actual video file.
