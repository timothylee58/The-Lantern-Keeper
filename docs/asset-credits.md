# Asset credits

The game's illustrations are original AI-generated artwork made for this project using OpenAI image generation. The paper-cut art direction uses indigo silhouettes, warm gold light and layered paper scenery. Earlier painted illustrations are retained only where needed for the preserved legacy adventure. They are not historical depictions or copies of traditional sacred patterns.

The interface cues in `public/audio` are original, locally synthesized sounds: `tap.wav` (a soft bell), `success.wav` (three rising notes), and `error.wav` (a gentle descending pair). They contain no samples, speech or recordings.

“Rain & Lanterns” (`rain-and-lanterns.wav`) is an original 60-second, 96 BPM fantasy soundtrack, composed and synthesized with `scripts/compose-hantu-bgm.py`. Soft rain, hollow bamboo-like percussion, plucked strings, airy flute, small gong-like tones and a frame-drum pulse evoke a playful night walk. The synthesized colours are inspired by regional instruments; they are not authentic performances, traditional compositions or replicas of a community's tuning system. No audio from the reference sources is copied. Sound defaults on for new visitors where browser policy allows, with a first-interaction fallback and persistent mute.

Instrument research: Singapore's National Heritage Board describes [Malay drum-making across Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Brunei](https://www.roots.gov.sg/ich-landing/ich/making-and-repairing-of-malay-drums); UNESCO describes [Indonesian angklung bamboo instruments](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/indonesian-angklung-00393) and the [gongs, percussion and flutes of gamelan](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/gamelan-another-indonesian-tradition-unesco-representative-list-intangible-cultural-heritage); Universiti Brunei Darussalam's [Rolling Heritage research](https://ias.ubd.edu.bn/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/working_paper_series_78.pdf) discusses gulintangan heritage. These overlapping instrumental colours do not imply one shared or interchangeable national tradition.

No externally sourced CC0 artwork or audio is included in this asset set. Font and software packages retain their own licenses. Folklore and cultural references are credited separately in the field guide; the neighbors and spirit encounters are fictional.

The reusable motion recipes were written for this project. The eight motion principles were informed by [Zajno Motion](https://motion.zajno.com/); no Zajno artwork, demonstration source, or proprietary plugin is bundled.
