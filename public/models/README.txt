LANTERN KEEPER — ORIGINAL CHARACTER MODEL PACK

15 original procedural puppet characters, each supplied as GLB 2.0 and DXF.
GLB: metres, +Y up, +Z forward, four seamless node-animation clips named Idle, Walk, Wave, Celebrate. Articulated rigid parts; not a skinned humanoid rig.
DXF: static assembled rest pose, 3DFACE geometry, millimetres, +Z up. Layers identify part and material. Open as a polygon mesh drawing, not editable parametric solids.
Character dimensions and individual filenames are in manifest.json.
No external artwork or meshes used. Friendly fictional spirit interpretations and everyday neighbor clothing; not authoritative cultural or religious representations.
To rebuild: python3 scripts/build-character-models.py in the project source.
